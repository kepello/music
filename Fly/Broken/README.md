_Broken People_

This song is not mine.  It was written by a friend, A'shon Franklin. We struck up a casual conversation while at a gathering with friends.  The topic turned to music and he shared that he has written some lyrics.  I offered to take a stab at putting them to music based on his vision.

Whate he sent me were not just "some lyrics"!  I find this song well written and moving.  I think you will enjoy it as well. Perhaps A'shon will read this and offer a better description of this song!
