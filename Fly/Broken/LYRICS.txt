_Broken People_

When you think about it, 
you and I the same.
Different faces, same ache, 
different chapters, same pain.
Looking for a name to blame 
for these emotional stains.
From Abel’s blood in the rain,
to Pharaoh breaking chains.

History got a way of repeating the strain.
Pressure on the mind, 
trying hard not to go insane.
So I’m hoping these words, 
reach a heart that’s open.
From the viewpoint, 
the voice of a person broken.

Life keep tapping on my shoulder,
Asking me to heal what I don’t wanna reopen.
Still I’m learning as I’m getting older,
Even faith feels heavy when you’re broken.

We’re just broken people,
Trying to love through the cracks.
Trying to trust while we’re carrying the past.
But Jehovah stays close,
When the pieces fall apart.
He’s never far from broken people.
Or a broken heart.

You ask what makes me broken?
It’s baggage from a “step”,
 that missed the runway.
Love crashed, 
we flew miles apart one day.
Hard to call her mother, 
harder still to explain.

And then a father,
who never saw what he had.
Too busy building dreams,
 in a bachelor pad.
I grew up in the echo,
 of what should’ve been.
Even though he changed, 
there’s a void deep within.
Late nights, no sleep, feeling weak—SWV
Still I choose forgiveness, 
that’s the way He wants me to be.
Thoughts once buried ,
now they floating in the open.
Yeah I’m healing… 
but I’m still broken.

We’re just broken people.
Trying to love through the cracks.
Trying to trust while we’re carrying the past.
But Jehovah stays close,
When the pieces fall apart.
He’s never far from broken people,
Or a broken heart.

What about you?
Learned pain way too young.
Physical, emotional—
before you could run.
A boy with his own hurt,
didn’t know how to cope.
So the pain he was in,
became pain he provoked.
Scars still pulling like a horse and a carriage.
Heart got a fence from surviving the damage.
Trust issues from men that couldn’t stay.
Tears in those eyes every time love walked away.
So hard to say goodbye to the one who should protect.
And then there’s me thinking I left grief at the check.
But it followed me home, yeah it followed me back.
Round and around till the heart starts to crack.

Seeds fall on rocky ground, still trying to grow,
Like turning a key but the engine won’t go.
Like a crown on a tooth that been chipped by the truth,
Still I believe restoration is proof.
Anything broken can be made whole again,
Even hearts, even love, even broken men.

So I’ll write it all out,
Try not to live in the past.
Watching life through a peephole,
As broken people pass.

We’re just broken people.
But we don’t walk alone,
Grace in the cracks, 
mercy in the bones.
Let’s trust in Jehovah,
And trust in each other’s arms.
Stay in love, keep it sacred.
Even under covers, safe from harm.

Drown out the noise they gave us.
Fathers, mothers, others who failed us.
Remember this truth when the night feels dark.
He’s never far from broken people.
Or a broken heart.
