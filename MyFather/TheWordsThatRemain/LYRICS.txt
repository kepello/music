[Verse 1]
She knew the road to Shiloh,
A journey made for prayer.
While hurt walked right beside her,
A burden she would bear.
Still she walked that way,
Still she came to pray.
She wouldn’t let another
Take her worship away.

[Verse 2]
She poured her heart to Jehovah,
With words no one could hear.
One who should have helped her
Saw wine where there were tears.
She answered him with kindness,
She simply told what was true.
Then left the weight with Jehovah—
The part that she could do.

[Chorus]
She rose and went her way,
Though sorrow still remained.
She left the weight with Jehovah,
And she was not the same.
Her heart still needed healing;
That healing would take time.
Peace came first at Shiloh—
Then joy began to shine.

[Verse 3]
Peace didn’t mean forgetting,
Or make the sorrow small.
It meant she wasn’t carrying
The burden of it all.
And as the months went passing,
Her wounded heart could heal.
Peace slowly flowered into joy—
Until joy again was real.

[Bridge]
The cruel words once spoken
Have faded with the years.
But we still read her prayers,
The ones she prayed through tears.
Others tried to name her
By what they thought they knew.
Jehovah heard her heart instead—
And showed us what was true.

[Final Chorus]
She rose and went her way,
Though sorrow still remained.
She left the weight with Jehovah,
And she was not the same.
And slowly came the healing,
As months went passing by.
The peace she found at Shiloh
Had flowered into delight.

[Outro]
Her lips once moved in silence,
Her words no one could hear.
But Jehovah heard each prayer—
And we still hold them dear.
