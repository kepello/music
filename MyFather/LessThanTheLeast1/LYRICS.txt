_Less Than the Least (1)_


[Verse 1]
I wore the garments of the righteous
Clean and pressed for all to see
Every thread of earned approval
Woven deep inside of me

I found honor in the highest seats
Spoke with bold authority
Certain Heaven stood beside me
Certain I could clearly see

[Verse 2]
Then the light exposed the fabric
Of the pride I used to wear
Every thread of self-assurance
Coming loose before me there

I had spoken loud of justice
With a hardened heart of stone
And the man who stood so certain
Saw how little he had known

[Chorus]
Strip away the pride I carried
Every title, every claim
Dress me now in gentle kindness
Shape my heart to humble ways

Let compassion be my covering
Patience stitched in every seam
I was high in my own glory
Now I’m less than the least

[Bridge]
I was certain I was faithful
Certain I could clearly see
But I was blind behind the honor
Pride dressed up as purity

Now I speak with softer language
Than the man I used to be
I have learned how pride can hide itself
Inside sincerity

[Verse 3]
So lay aside the robes of judgment
And the need to stand above
Clothe yourselves in tender mercies
Quiet strength and patient love

We are smallest when we’re reaching
For the praise of other men
But the lowly learn compassion
When they kneel instead of stand

[Final Chorus]
Strip away the pride we carry
Every title, every claim
Dress us now in gentle kindness
Shape our hearts to humble ways

Let compassion be our covering
Patience stitched in every seam
May we learn, like Paul before us
To be less than the least

[Outro]
Less than the least
Less than the least
May we learn, like Paul before us
To be less than the least
