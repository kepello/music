Where do I even start?

The empty chair feels safer,
Than waiting on the air to change.
Distance becomes a protection,
If love and fear -- are raised the same

People speak of You as Father,

I can hear the peace within it,

Kindness was always temporary,
And good things left too soon.

Certain love -- would leave the room.

[Chorus]
