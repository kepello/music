_Patch Release_

Patch Notes imagines personal growth through the lens of a software release. Anyone who works in technology knows the language: bug fixes, feature creep, refactoring, legacy code. But the same ideas apply surprisingly well to life. We accumulate habits, assumptions, and “temporary fixes” that somehow become permanent. Every once in a while it’s worth stepping back, cleaning up the system, and shipping a better version.

The song plays with that idea in a light-hearted way. Instead of dramatic self-reflection, it frames growth as an upgrade cycle: closing bugs, archiving legacy code, deleting old hacks, and pushing a cleaner build to production. Musically, it leans into a relaxed funk groove — something confident and playful rather than heavy. The goal was to make it feel like the moment when a big system finally deploys successfully: calm, satisfying, and maybe just a little triumphant.

The hook says it all: “Hard reboot — beast mode.” Sometimes the best upgrade is simply starting fresh.
