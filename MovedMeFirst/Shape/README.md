_The Shape of Who I Am_

I’ve always had a restless mind. Sometimes that shows up as taking things apart to see how they work. Sometimes it’s chasing ideas or patterns that might lead somewhere interesting. And sometimes it’s just sitting with a guitar and letting a thought turn into a melody. Over time, I’ve noticed that many of the paths I’ve taken started that way — with a question that refused to stay quiet.

Looking back, those questions have shaped more than just projects or hobbies. They’ve shaped how I see the world and how I understand myself. The things we build, the things we explore, and the choices we make often reflect the way our minds are wired.

Along the way there are also the things that ground us — friendships, faith, experience, and the slow wisdom that comes with time. They remind us that we’re not walking these roads alone and that not every mystery needs to be solved immediately.

In the end, this song isn’t really about having answers. It’s about learning to live with curiosity, and about the slow discovery that happens when you keep following good questions wherever they lead. Piece by piece, those questions reveal something unexpected: the shape of who you are becoming.
