Music moved me first. Long before I could do anything about it, it was already doing something to me.

What changed is that I started trying to answer. After spending real effort learning to write lyrics and arrange songs, I found myself engaging with music directly rather than only receiving it—leaning in instead of back.

That turned out to be a different kind of exposure than listening ever was. Writing this way meant putting down things I wouldn't normally speak about, and then leaving them written.

The title track is where that shows most plainly. "Music Moved Me First" appears four times on this album—the same lyrics, word for word, arranged four different ways. The words held still while I kept turning them over, looking for the setting that told the truth about them.
