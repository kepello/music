_Moved Me First (2)_

After the first version, I wanted to hear what would happen if I took almost everything away.

No swirling organ. No biting guitar bends. No theatrical build. Just one guitar and one voice—deep, soft, airy, intimate.

In some ways, this may be the closest of the four versions to how I would have written the song sitting alone with a guitar in my hands, without AI-augmented help. Stripped back, clear and clean. Nothing between the words and what they mean to me.

The first arrangement was written to audibly move me. I wanted to hear the music reach out and grab me—the very experience the song describes.

This one was written to move my heart.

And it does.

Without the production surrounding the lyrics, some of the lines became more personal than I realized they were when I wrote them.

I thought I was only listening
Turns out there’s something more.

With just a guitar and a quiet voice, that doesn’t feel like a declaration anymore. It feels almost like an admission.

And:

I’m allowed to touch it
Shape it
Make it stay.

Those words mean something different when there isn’t much else happening. There’s no place for them to hide. It’s simply me, a guitar, and this relatively new realization that something which has moved me for my entire life is now something I can reach toward and participate in.

The other arrangements may do more musically.

This one needs less.

Of the four versions, this is the one that touches my heart the most.

Maybe because underneath everything else—the technology, the arrangements, the possibilities—this is probably where I would have found the song on my own.

Sitting with a guitar.

Quietly answering back.
