
_Exquisite Delight_

These verses are dear to many of us. They remind us that we hold onto promises from God about a future that is almost impossible to imagine — and yet we love to try. In thinking about those promises, we begin to realize something wonderful: we don’t have to wait for them to be fulfilled to experience joy. We can find great, even exquisite, delight right now by becoming friends with someone who not only wishes such good things for us, but who is already preparing them.

Before anything else, I should say that this musical setting may not be to everyone’s liking.

While experimenting with different musical styles, I stumbled into this one almost by accident. I’ve always enjoyed the sound of older rock music from the 1960s and 70s — especially the guitar. There’s something about the almost psychedelic tone of a wah pedal and an overdriven tube amplifier, where the sound breaks up in beautifully raw ways. The groove and tone of that era have always brought a smile to my face.

For me, that sense of delight fits these verses.

I also realize that hearing cherished scriptural passages presented in this style may feel unusual — even jarring — to some listeners. That’s understandable. But for me, the joy and groove of this sound capture something of the delight these verses describe. If it causes someone else to smile the way it makes me smile, then the experiment was well worth it.
