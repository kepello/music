_Friends Born for the Storm_

This song grew out of the thought expressed at Proverbs 17:17:
“A true friend shows love at all times, and is a brother who is born for times of distress.”

At first the idea seems simple: when someone we care about faces distress, a real friend rises to the occasion. When the ground gives way and things fall apart, a true friend draws closer rather than pulling away. In that sense, true friends are born for the storm.

But the thought keeps unfolding, to me.

In times of distress we often find that strength comes from more than one place. Alongside loyal friends, we discover the steady support of God himself. Often quietly and without spectacle, he proves to be the most dependable friend of all — the one who does not let us fall when everything else feels uncertain.

And there is another source of strength that many of us recognize. Words we have known for years — passages from the Scriptures — can suddenly take on deeper meaning when life becomes difficult. Like an old friend who understands exactly what to say, those words seem to rise to the occasion just when they are needed most.

This song reflects on those three sources of strength:
friends who stay close, God who holds us steady, and the enduring words that give us courage when the heart is worn.

Together, they remind us that when distress comes, we are not facing the storm alone.
