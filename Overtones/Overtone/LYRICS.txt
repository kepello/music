
In the quiet before the sun,
When the world has not begun,
There’s a whisper in Your Word
That I scarcely let be heard.

Low and steady, line by line,
Turning phrases over time,
Murmuring what You have shown,
Like a seed that’s softly sown.

What begins in undertone
Doesn’t stay there on its own,
But Your Word is more than light,
More than logic polished bright.

It asks the soul to sing it twice—
Once in whisper, once in life;
What was planted, slowly grown,
Rises now in overtone.

I have lived inside my head,
Blueprint thoughts and words unsaid,
Measured faith through lines and forms,
Weathered truth through structured storms.

Understanding every part,
Still unformed within the heart,
Till the murmur, soft but strong,
Called my knowing into song.

What begins in undertone
Doesn’t stay there on its own,
But Your Word is more than light,
More than logic polished bright.

It asks the soul to sing it twice—
Once in whisper, -- once in life;
What was planted, slowly grown,
Rises now -- in overtone.

Let it linger where it lands,
Working deeper than I planned.
Let it move from mind to bone,
From quiet murmur into tone.

Word to breth and breth to song,
Where the hidden truths belong—
Day and night, in undertone,
Faith takes root in overtone.

What began in undertone
Now resounds in what has grown,
Meditation turned to praise,
Whispered faith reshapes my days.

From the hidden roots below,
Quiet waters overflow—
What was planted, slowly grown,
Flourishes --  in overtone.
