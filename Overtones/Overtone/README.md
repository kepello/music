_Overtone_

Psalm 1:2 describes delight in God’s Word as a quiet, continual meditation — reading in an undertone. This song reflects on what that kind of meditation produces. Truth held gently and repeatedly does not remain abstract; it forms roots.

This started from a few lines that were written first, resonated deeply with me and became a foundation in the chorus:

    But Your Word is more than light,
    More than logic polished bright.

    It asks the soul to sing it twice—
    Once in whisper, -- once in life;

The overtone is not performance, but resonance. What is murmured inwardly becomes embodied outwardly. The song is less about proclamation and more about formation — how Scripture, when allowed to settle deeply, reshapes the one who dwells in it.
