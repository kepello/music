_Finishing That Line_

Nearly thirty years ago I started writing a poem but never finished it. Only one line stayed with me all that time:

“For words to be written must first be defined; And this in truth is a fear of mine.”

For years I assumed it lingered because it sounded clever — a line that deserved a poem I could never quite write. But recently I realized something different. The line stayed with me because it was true for me. It named a fear I wasn’t ready to face.

I didn’t finish the poem because I wasn’t ready to write the next line. Putting words to buried thoughts and emotions can hurt. Sometimes it feels like cleaning a wound — it stings at first, and you wonder why you’re doing it. But that pain is part of the healing.

Lately I’ve been discovering the power of writing honestly about those things. The hurt doesn’t disappear, but it begins to heal instead of quietly festering.

I once heard the phrase, “All good things in life are on the other side of hard.” This song is about discovering that truth for myself.

I’m not finished yet. But I feel like I’m finally finishing that line.
