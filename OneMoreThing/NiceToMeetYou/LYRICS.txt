_Nice to Meet You_

[Verse 1]
"Nice to meet you,”
Said a thousand times,
Talk about little things,
Lose track of time.

People come and go,
Only a few will stay.
Some become old friends,
Most go a different way,

But this one's kind'a different,
A little awkward, I can't lie.
But I found myself think'n
Seems like a decent guy.

[Chorus]
“Nice to meet you,” “Nice to meet you,”
Know all the words by heart.
Still awkward at the first,
What to say; where to start.

Said the words a thousand times,
Never knowing what would come.
Always left to wonder
What might have just begun.

[Verse 2]
The more I get to know ya,
The more familiar ya seem,
Things about your story,
Feel like memories.

See some old defenses,
Know why they were there.
Everything you're tell'in  me
Is something we have shared.

Found some things I like in you,
Some that I would change,
But knowing where you come from
Made them easy to explain.

[Bridge]
You’ve been here my whole life,
Yet somehow we never met.
Not really introduced,
Never took the time, I guess.

I've often felt disappointed, 
And never called you friend.
But today,  flaws and all, 
I have to say,...  I like the man I am.

[Final Chorus]
“Nice to meet you,” “Nice to meet you,”
Even knowing all the words by heart,
Sure was Awkward at the first,
Wondering where to start.

Said it a thousand times,
But this time was a first,
To say "Nice to meet you"
To myself, and know it's true.

[Instrumental]

[Outro]
Yes, this time was a first,
To say "Nice to meet you"
