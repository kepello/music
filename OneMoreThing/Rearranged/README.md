_Rearranged_

This song was written during a moment of uncertainty in my work life. Not a crisis, not a breaking point—just one of those stretches where the ground shifts and you realize you may have to begin again, even after years of effort.

The feeling wasn’t anger or disappointment. It was something quieter: the recognition that progress doesn’t always accumulate the way we expect. Sometimes the clock resets, the room changes shape, and you find yourself being measured again.

The song isn’t really about a job. It’s about patience—about standing still long enough to understand whether you should stay where you are or step into something new. When both doors seem possible, the hardest part isn’t choosing; it’s waiting until the right choice becomes clear.
